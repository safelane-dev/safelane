<p className="eyebrow">Approve once. SafeLane runs the release.</p>

<p className="lead">SafeLane checks the exact artifact, recommends a rollout, and asks for one approval. It then coordinates your existing Argo Rollout to completion or a safe stop.</p>

<div className="trust-strip">
  <div><strong>Exact release</strong><span>Source, build, and image match</span></div>
  <div><strong>One approval</strong><span>Valid for one release only</span></div>
  <div><strong>Small patch</strong><span>Image and canary steps only</span></div>
  <div><strong>Argo health</strong><span>Your analysis stays in control</span></div>
</div>

## One request. One approval. One complete release.

<div className="path-grid">
  <div className="path-step"><span>01 · CHECK</span><strong>Identify the release</strong><p>SafeLane compares the candidate with the version that runs now.</p></div>
  <div className="path-step"><span>02 · DECIDE</span><strong>Recommend a lane</strong><p>SafeLane shows the evidence, health checks, and rollout steps.</p></div>
  <div className="path-step"><span>03 · RELEASE</span><strong>Stay with Argo</strong><p>SafeLane monitors each gate until the release stops or completes.</p></div>
</div>
